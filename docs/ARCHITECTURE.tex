\begin{abstract}
This document describes the current production architecture. The active trading path is the JEPA event-option static-union package for 0DTE SPXW, QQQ, and SPY options. The older Hybrid Attention-MLP, GBT+RL, level-stability, structural-profile, OptionValue, and 180 minute JEPA systems remain in the repository as research or legacy tooling, but they are not the active live contract unless a service file explicitly points to them.
\end{abstract}

\section{Active Runtime}

The live trading stack has two cooperating services:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{lX}
\toprule
Component & Role \\
\midrule
\path{services/realtime_feed.py} & Downloads and normalizes live spot and option-chain data. It writes 0DTE snapshots and feature files under \path{rt_data/YYYYMMDD/}. \\
\path{bots/tradingbot_wrapper_jepa.py} & Loads the event-option policy and component registry, scores live candidates, manages open positions, and emits paper order intents. \\
\path{systemd/realtime_feed.service} & Production unit for the feed. \\
\path{systemd/ai_bot.service} & Production unit for the bot. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

The bot is an alert and paper-intent process. It does not submit broker orders.
