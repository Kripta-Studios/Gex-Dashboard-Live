\begin{abstract}
This document catalogues the feature blocks used by the current system.  It is intentionally more detailed than a summary report: it explains the economic meaning of the key engineered features, the state/input split used by XInputJEPA, the 42 JEPA features appended downstream, and the option-candidate features used by the 0DTE research models.  The machine-readable source of truth remains the model artifacts and feature-name JSON files.
\end{abstract}

\section{Key Engineered Market Features}

\subsection{Net Greek Exposure}

These are the core state variables.  The important features are:

\begin{longtable}{p{0.23\textwidth}p{0.67\textwidth}}
\toprule
Feature & Interpretation \\
\midrule
\endhead
\path{net_gamma} & Aggregate gamma exposure.  It describes how dealer delta changes with spot.  The sign and magnitude condition whether price movement is expected to be dampened or amplified. \\
\path{net_vanna} & Aggregate vanna exposure.  It connects volatility movement to delta hedging pressure.  It becomes especially relevant when IV is moving. \\
\path{net_charm} & Aggregate charm exposure.  It captures delta decay through time, particularly important for 0DTE because time decay is intraday and fast. \\
\path{net_dgex} & Delta-gamma exposure proxy.  It is used as a directional hedging-pressure descriptor and as a source of DGEX magnet distances. \\
\path{net_zomma} & Gamma sensitivity to volatility.  It helps distinguish gamma regimes that are stable from regimes where volatility changes can reshape gamma quickly. \\
\path{net_delta} & Aggregate directional option exposure.  It is a direct state descriptor but is most useful in interaction with gamma and vanna. \\
\path{net_vega} & Aggregate volatility exposure.  It conditions option payoff and volatility sensitivity. \\
\path{net_vomma} & Convexity of vega.  It describes whether volatility exposure itself changes sharply as IV moves. \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\subsection{Weekly Exposure Features}

The weekly fields mirror the 0DTE fields:

\begin{center}
\path{wk_net_gamma}, \path{wk_net_vanna}, \path{wk_net_charm}, \path{wk_net_dgex}, \path{wk_net_zomma}, \path{wk_net_delta}, \path{wk_net_vega}, \path{wk_net_vomma}.

They exist because 0DTE exposure alone can be too local.  Weekly exposure can define broader support/resistance or magnetic zones.  Ratio fields such as \path{gamma_0dte_vs_wk}, \path{vanna_0dte_vs_wk}, \path{dgex_0dte_vs_wk}, \path{delta_0dte_vs_wk}, \path{vega_0dte_vs_wk}, and \path{vomma_0dte_vs_wk} compare short-expiry pressure to the wider expiry structure.

\subsection{Regime and Boolean State Features}

Examples:

\begin{center}
\path{gamma_regime}, \path{vanna_bullish}, \path{charm_bullish}, \path{dgex_sticky}, \path{zomma_stabilizing}, \path{wk_gamma_regime}, \path{wk_vanna_bullish}, \path{wk_dgex_sticky}, \path{wk_zomma_stabilizing}.

These are not meant to be final trade rules.  They are compact regime descriptors.  A GBT can use them to learn different behavior in positive/negative gamma, sticky DGEX, or stabilizing zomma environments.

\section{Feature Interpretation Caveats}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item A feature name can be economically meaningful without being individually predictive.
  \item A JEPA latent coordinate can be predictive without being human-interpretable.
  \item The GBT may ignore most features.  That is acceptable if the retained splits improve OOS performance.
  \item Option candidate labels are noisier than direction labels because option payoff includes spread, theta, IV, gamma, and liquidity.
  \item Oracle-derived fields must never enter deployable features.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Conclusion}

The key feature blocks are the net Greek state, strike geometry, volatility regime, intraday location, JEPA latent state/input, and option candidate path state.  The evidence supports using JEPA features as a complement to the base GBT features.  The next feature-development frontier is not more static market columns; it is better live-safe option-path and liquidity descriptors that can help a learned option selector beat fixed delta.
