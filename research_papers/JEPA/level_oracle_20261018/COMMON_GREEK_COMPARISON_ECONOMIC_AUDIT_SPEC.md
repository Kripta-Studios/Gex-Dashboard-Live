# Independent common-Greek comparison economic audit

The auditor reconstructs the fixed `cue_net__0` versus
`common_greek_net__0` comparison from sealed scores without importing the
producer scheduler, metrics or inference code. Both policies use strict USD
threshold zero. There is no selection, calibration fit or rescue policy.

For every state, rank by descending score and lower action ID. WAIT and NO_FILL
advance one minute; NO_FILL records exactly one attempt and cannot substitute a
runner-up; FILLED blocks through `exit_i` and resumes at `exit_i+1`. Independently
reconstruct attempts, fill ledgers, base/adverse economics, daily coverage and
the four fixed calibration populations for all 750 ticker-days in 2025 and 369
in 2026H1. The six missing source days remain `NO_EVALUABLE`.

All 1,113 available control-score arrays, including the nine short sessions,
must equal the completed and independently audited compact comparison in action
IDs, state IDs, dtype, shape and score bytes. Each new prediction marker must
state `EXACT_AUDITED_CUE_ALL_SESSIONS` and precede labels. The 2026 prediction
markers must bind the exact `test_freeze.json`, which in turn binds the complete
2025 report and marker without choosing a winner.

The audit verifies the SessionClock audit/index and the implementation review's
six authorities. It verifies `training_provenance.json` against the freeze and
rehashes every nested TRAIN input once at admission and again before PASS. It
also rehashes all consumed baseline predictions, actions, labels, reports,
markers and global completion members. Synthetic fixtures cover the scheduler,
clocks, missingness, calibration, marker membership, post-read mutation and
nested TRAIN-provenance mutation.

A PASS concerns economic execution given sealed predictions. It does not refit
either model or reproduce inference, does not make 2025/2026 prospective, and
does not authorize promotion.
